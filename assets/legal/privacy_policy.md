# Privacy Policy for AI Voice Genie

**Last Updated:** July 2026  
**Effective Date:** July 2026

---

## 1. 🛡️ Introduction & Commitment

Welcome to **AI Voice Genie** ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting your personal data and digital footprint. This Privacy Policy explains in transparent terms how we collect, use, transmit, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application and voice-enabled AI services.

---

## 2. 📊 Information We Collect

To provide an intelligent, voice-driven, and multimodal assistant experience, we collect only essential categories of data:

- **Account Authentication Data:** When you sign in using Google Firebase Authentication, we collect basic profile identifiers, including your email address, display name, and secure authentication tokens.
- **Voice & Audio Recordings:** When you tap the microphone to speak, we capture audio recordings solely for real-time speech-to-text transcription.
- **Files & Media Attachments:** Photos, images, and PDF documents you attach to messages for AI vision and document analysis.
- **Text Prompts & Chat History:** The text messages, queries, and instructions you submit during your conversations.
- **Device & Performance Metrics:** Basic diagnostic information (OS version, device model, and error crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure app stability and debug issues.

---

## 3. ⚙️ How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly to operate and enhance your personal assistant experience:
- **Speech Transcription:** Converting spoken audio input into accurate text prompts.
- **AI Response Generation:** Processing your text queries, attached images, and documents to generate intelligent answers.
- **Account Security:** Authenticating your session and synchronizing your preferences safely across devices.
- **App Optimization:** Identifying bugs, optimizing response latency, and improving user interface responsiveness.

---

## 4. 🤖 Third-Party AI Providers (Crucial)

To power advanced reasoning, vision, and conversation capabilities, **AI Voice Genie** transmits your user input data (text prompts, transcribed audio, images, and PDF documents) to industry-leading Artificial Intelligence providers. By using the app, you explicitly consent to secure data transmission with:

- **Google LLC:** Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore storage, and Google Gemini APIs for multimodal processing.
- **OpenAI, LLC:** GPT models for advanced natural language understanding and response generation.
- **Anthropic, PBC:** Claude models for structured reasoning and document analysis.

> 🔒 **Data Protection Guarantee:** We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertisers. The AI providers listed above process your data strictly to fulfill your active requests and operate under stringent API enterprise data privacy agreements that prohibit using API data to train their foundational models.

---

## 5. 🔐 Device Permissions

You maintain full control over device permissions and can revoke them at any time in your system settings:

- **Microphone:** Required only when actively recording voice prompts for speech recognition.
- **Camera & Photo Library:** Required only when taking a photo or selecting an image/document attachment to send to the AI.

---

## 6. 🗑️ Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to control your personal data:
- **Chat History:** You can clear or delete individual chat conversations directly within the app at any time.
- **Account Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated Firebase authentication data instantly using the in-app **"Delete Account"** button in your Profile settings.
- **Manual Requests:** You may also email us directly to request full data purging.
